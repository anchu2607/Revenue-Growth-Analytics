Revenue Growth Analysis Dashboard – Power BI Project
🚀 A Data-Driven Approach to Sales & Profitability Insights

📌 Project Overview
This Power BI dashboard analyzes revenue growth trends across different dimensions, including sales by category, sub-category, region, segment, and payment modes. The dashboard provides actionable insights into sales performance, profit margins, and return rates, enabling businesses to make informed decisions.

🎯 Key Objectives
✔ Track total revenue growth across multiple years and regions.
✔ Analyze sales by category, sub-category, and payment methods.
✔ Monitor profit trends and forecast future sales growth.
✔ Identify high-performing and underperforming product segments.
✔ Improve business decision-making with real-time insights.

🛠 Tech Stack & Tools
✅ Power BI – Interactive dashboards & data visualization.
✅ SQL – Data extraction & transformation.
✅ Excel – Data preprocessing & forecasting analysis.

📊 Data Pipeline & Processing
1️⃣ Data Collection & Cleaning (SQL & Excel)
Data is sourced from sales records, customer transactions, and profit margins.
SQL queries are used for data extraction, transformation, and preprocessing.
SELECT 
    Order_Date, Product_Category, Product_SubCategory, 
    Sales_Amount, Profit, Quantity, Ship_Mode, Payment_Method
FROM Sales_Data
WHERE Order_Date BETWEEN '2019-01-01' AND '2023-12-31';
2️⃣ Data Analysis & Insights
✔ Sales by Category & Sub-Category – Identifies top-selling products.
✔ Revenue by Region & Ship Mode – Analyzes trends based on shipping preferences.
✔ Monthly & Yearly Sales & Profit Trends – Tracks seasonal variations.
✔ State-wise & Segment-wise Profitability – Highlights business opportunities.

3️⃣ Power BI Dashboard Features
📌 Total Sales & Profit Overview
📌 Sales by Category & Sub-Category
📌 State-Wise Sales & Profit Heatmap
📌 Monthly & Yearly Growth Trends
📌 Sales by Ship Mode & Payment Method
📌 15-Day Revenue Growth Forecasting

📊 Visuals Used:
✔ Bar Charts – Category-wise & regional sales comparison.
✔ Line Charts – Monthly & yearly revenue trends.
✔ Pie Charts – Payment mode & shipping preference distribution.
✔ Heatmaps – State-wise profit & loss distribution.
✔ Forecasting Models – 15-day revenue prediction.

📈 Key Insights & Findings
🔹 Technology & Office Supplies contributed to the highest revenue, exceeding $600K annually.
🔹 Standard Class shipping accounted for over 50% of total sales.
🔹 January & March showed peak sales activity, indicating seasonal trends.
🔹 Regions like New York & Texas generated the highest profit margins.
🔹 Online payment methods were preferred by 52% of customers, reducing cash transactions.
